Compass Spin

One spin, 20 seconds long. The needle starts at north and receives 2,001 random pushes, about 100 a second, each nudging it 1.5° clockwise or counter-clockwise. Choose your direction, then hold the intention for the whole spin.

Pick a direction to begin.

How it works

The needle starts at north (the gold mark). Over 20 seconds your browser's cryptographic generator draws 2,001 random bits, about 100 a second. Each bit pushes the needle 1.5° one way or the other, so you watch it wander. The arc shows its net travel from north. Your score is the number of pushes that went your way out of 2,001. Chance expects 1,000.5, and the needle's final position is exactly your score drawn as an angle: every push your way above 1,000.5 moves it 3° further onto your side. Pushes cancel out, so 6 one way and 5 back is a net of 1. With an odd number of pushes it can never end exactly on north. A z-score of 1.65 or more (about 37 pushes above chance, the needle 110° onto your side) happens by chance only 5% of the time.
