How it works

Each of the 25 cards shows one of five symbols: circle, cross, waves, square or star. In Clairvoyance mode, all 25 targets are drawn before you start and stay hidden. In Precognition mode, each target is drawn only after your guess is recorded. Every draw uses your browser's cryptographic random generator. Guessing alone averages 5 hits; 9 or more happens by chance less than 5% of the time. Keys 1 to 5 also work.
