Remote Viewing

A target scene is chosen at random and sealed before you begin. Describe it, then rank four candidates by how well they match. Ranking the true target first is a direct hit; chance is one in four.

Write shapes, colours, textures, temperature and sounds. Avoid naming things too early. Sketch if it helps.

Tap the scenes from best match to worst. The first one you tap is rank 1.

Running it as an experimenter

For a stricter session, the experimenter starts the session and reads the seal aloud or writes it down, then leaves the room or stays silent. The viewer records impressions and does the ranking. On reveal, the page shows the secret salt and target, so anyone can recompute the SHA-256 seal and confirm the target wasn't changed after the fact. The leaderboard ranks viewers by direct hits across all their sessions, with at least 3 sessions to qualify.
