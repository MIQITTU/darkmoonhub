Move the Line

A generator produces 2,000 random bits over 20 seconds. Each 1 moves the line up, each 0 moves it down. Left alone, it wanders around the centre. Push it up.

The dashed curves mark the 95% band. Ending above the top curve is significant.
How it works

Each bit is drawn from your browser's cryptographic generator, 100 a second. Your score is the number of ones out of 2,000; chance expects 1,000 with a standard deviation of about 22. Keep your attention on the line and hold the intention for it to rise. Don't strain; most people who score well describe a calm, steady expectation.
