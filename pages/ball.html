Ball Machine

You start with 100 credits and 20 balls. Pick a side before each drop. Every peg sends the ball left or right at random. If it lands on your side you win 10 credits; if not, you lose 10.

How it works

The ball passes nine rows of pegs, and at each one a cryptographic random bit sends it left or right. It lands in one of ten bins: the five on the left or the five on the right. The odds are exactly even, so a machine left to chance ends the game near 100 credits. Your leaderboard score is the number of balls that landed on your side.
